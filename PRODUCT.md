# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Mixed audience: fellow researchers and collaborators who will read carefully and want depth, alongside curious non-specialists who want to understand the work at a surface level. The design must work at both depths: scannable first impression, richly rewarding on closer reading.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Matthew Lucia: synthetic biologist, computational researcher, composer, and engineer. Showcases research in artificial cells and Pdu microcompartment protein engineering, ongoing projects (Neuroaesthetic Music), lab resource tools, a microscopy gallery, and music (original compositions and cello performance). The site should communicate that serious scientific work and creative output can coexist in one person.

## Brand Personality

Curious, layered, unexpected. The site should feel like a mind that moves between disciplines: precise but not sterile, rigorous but not academic, creative but not precious. It rewards the reader who looks twice.

## Anti-references

- Academic lab pages: cluttered, link-heavy, no visual hierarchy, the typical university department site
- VC-backed startup landing pages: purple gradients, glassmorphism, hero metrics, "revolutionize" copy
- Science Twitter/X aesthetic: preprint-dump, no curation, no narrative

## Design Principles

1. **Curiosity rewarded at depth.** The first read is fast and clear. The second read reveals texture: annotations, layered information, unexpected structural choices.
2. **Disciplines in productive tension.** The design should hold science and music in the same frame without flattening either. The unexpected connects them.
3. **Confident restraint.** Bold choices are made deliberately, not decoratively. Every element earns its place, but the place is not always obvious.
4. **Asymmetry as intent.** Off-centre, staggered, and interrupted layouts signal a mind that doesn't default to the obvious arrangement.
5. **The engineer's instinct.** Built from first principles. Nothing is there because it is conventional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. High contrast between text and background at all section transitions (dark/light alternating). Reduced motion respected via `prefers-reduced-motion`. No information conveyed by color alone.
