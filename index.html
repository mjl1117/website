Synthetic Biology

Artificial Cells

Engineering minimal living systems from lipid vesicles and purified molecular machinery. Reconstituting gene expression, metabolism, and division inside synthetic membrane compartments to understand the fundamental principles of life.

Protein Engineering

Pdu Microcompartment Proteins

Engineering bacterial microcompartment (BMC) shell proteins from the Pdu system to create synthetic nanopores and protein assemblies. Combining computational docking and molecular dynamics to design protein–membrane interfaces for controlled transport.

PduA / PduB BMC shells Nanopore design Molecular docking MD simulation
