Synthetic Biology

Artificial Cells

Engineering minimal living systems from lipid vesicles and purified molecular machinery. Reconstituting gene expression, metabolism, and division inside synthetic membrane compartments to understand the fundamental principles of life.

Lipid vesicles Cell-free TXTL Bottom-up assembly Minimal cells

Protein Engineering

Pdu Microcompartment Proteins

Engineering bacterial microcompartment shell proteins from the Pdu system to create synthetic nanopores. Combining computational docking and molecular dynamics to design protein-membrane interfaces for controlled transport.

PduA / PduB BMC shells Nanopore design MD simulation
