Synthetic Biology

Artificial Cells

Engineering minimal living systems from lipid vesicles and purified molecular machinery. Reconstituting cellular processes — gene expression, metabolism, division — inside synthetic membrane compartments to probe the principles of life.

Protein Engineering

EutM Synthetic Pores

Harnessing the bacterial microcompartment shell protein EutM to engineer selective nanopores. Combining computational docking and molecular dynamics to design protein assemblies with defined pore geometries for membrane transport applications.

EutM · Pore engineering · Molecular docking · MD simulation
